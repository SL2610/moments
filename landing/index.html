How it works

Three steps, no manual tagging.

The same flow whether you're running it on a laptop under the DJ booth or a $0 cloud server.

01 / upload

Add the photos

Drag in a few hundred photos, or bulk-import a whole folder from the photographer. Everything's stored on your own disk.

02 / scan

The AI scans every face

A background worker detects and fingerprints every face in every photo. It runs on your machine — nothing is uploaded anywhere to do this.

03 / find

Guests find themselves

One link, a password, a selfie. Guests browse the shared album, tag themselves, and download a zip of exactly the photos they're in.
